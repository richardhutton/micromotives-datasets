By source and status

What the statuses mean

  • BuiltAlready in the corpus, checked and on disk.
  • BuildableNot built. The condition text, the outcome and the record of who saw which condition all appear to exist. Still gets the full check when built.
  • Needs checkingNot built. Something must be read or found first; the row says what.
  • UnlikelyNot built. Probably breaks one of our rules (an image condition, no shared outcome, needs US knowledge, no data); the row says which.

Novelty (0 to 0.33) is how far a candidate's title sits from the nearest built title. Higher means less like anything we already hold.
