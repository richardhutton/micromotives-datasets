Category Coverage

Where the experiments we have built sit across eight categories, in the text set, the ResearchBox set (consumer and pricing studies, kept apart so they can be left out of training) and the image set, and where the candidates still on our lists would add to them.

Built so far

How to read this

  • Every experiment uses the same eight categories, the six TESS uses for its own studies and two political ones added for the UK layer. Text experiments keep the category recorded in the experiment list. Image experiments were given theirs on 13 Nov 2026 from each title and description, so read those as a first pass.
  • Candidates are experiments not built yet. For text that means Buildable or Needs checking; for images it also includes Waiting for images. Anything marked Unlikely is left out.
  • Jev, our model judge, was asked separately which category fits each experiment from its title and description. Where it disagrees with the recorded category the list shows its suggestion and confidence; nothing changes until a person decides. Innovation Panel experiments are not sent for the category check. They are sent for the kind check (titles from UKDS's public documentation and our own descriptions only, on rich's ruling of 19 Nov 2026).
  • The SocSci210 paper (Kolluri et al., 2025, Figure 2) sorts its 210 studies into eight disciplines but does not say which study sits where, so this page cannot set our spread beside theirs.
