How to read this

  • Every experiment uses the same eight categories, the six TESS uses for its own studies and two political ones added for the UK layer. Text experiments keep the category recorded in the experiment list. Image experiments were given theirs on 13 Nov 2026 from each title and description, so read those as a first pass.
  • Candidates are experiments not built yet. For text that means Buildable or Needs checking; for images it also includes Waiting for images. Anything marked Unlikely is left out.
  • The SocSci210 paper (Kolluri et al., 2025, Figure 2) sorts its 210 studies into eight disciplines but does not say which study sits where, so this page cannot set our spread beside theirs.
