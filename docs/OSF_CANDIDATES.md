# OSF candidates (UK survey experiments)

A sweep of OSF on 18 Oct 2026: about 1,390 projects searched, about 120 read, the data file
opened for about 45. Nothing here is built. Every project was opened; "Data" is the file
actually read.

## The two UK checks

1. **Sample is UK** — read from the deposit or paper (a fact, not a judgement).
   UK = UK-only sample. Mixed = the UK is one of several countries (use the UK subset).
   Check = not confirmed from the data.
2. **Content is appropriate for the UK** — Jev `score_uk_priority` on the public title,
   project description and outcomes (no respondent data). Two scores, each 0–4:
   - **UK relevance**: 0 of no interest to UK research or policy … 4 prominent in UK debate.
   - **Current**: 0 tied to a past event or superseded … 4 timeless.
   A score with low confidence (< 0.6) is marked ⚑ for a person to decide.

## Where the two checks and the sweep disagree — for you to decide

- **Brexit studies (utwd7, b9762):** UK-relevant but scored as dated (0.4, 0.8): the
  question is tied to the 2016–2020 period.
- **COVID misinformation (cxwvp):** dated (1.1) and pandemic-framed.
- **be8a2 (digital imprints):** the sweep rates it good, but Jev rates it partly dated (1.6)
  with no confidence at all, so this needs a person's reading. The imprint law dates from 2023.
- **Mixed samples (mu7gs, vmbr9, 2xgc6, dncmh, 2qzmb):** the content is UK-appropriate, but
  only the UK subset could be used.
- **Not confirmed as UK (bd2jk, utwd7, dcey2, jb43f):** check the country column before building.

## Poor (about 45, not listed in full)

These failed a criterion: no data deposited (most often only a pre-analysis plan), no
demographics, no randomised arm, a live interaction rather than a fixed stimulus, or a
non-UK sample. The full list is in the sweep report.

## Patterns

- **Topics:** climate and energy, food and alcohol labels, gambling messages, political
  ads and transparency, Brexit.
- **Most common gap:** UK political-science experiments put only a pre-analysis plan on
  OSF, with the data elsewhere or "on request".
- **Second gap:** the data is deposited without the stimulus text or images.
- **Licence:** most have none set, so ask the authors, as with the image studies.
